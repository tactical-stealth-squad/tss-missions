v1.16
====
- New feature where you can now spawn as many enemy AI squads as you want through your action menu.

- Increased the Garrison Strength, allowing you to spawn and garrison a minimum of 8 and a maximum of 100 enemy soldiers from a faction of your choice.
- New, you can now turn off the garrison if you wish.

- Optimized garrisoning code, removing the initial freeze/lag at the start of the mission.
Now garrisoning is done in a more efficient and faster manner. 

- New MP parameter for the player stats to the player: none, HUD, mouse action (hint)

- Updated MP parameters, giving them some more details to their descriptions.
- Fixed/Updated the save/load loadout function with arsenal now all your loadout should save and load.
- Updated the briefing removing the mission section.
